# Session Study: protocol draft

**Status:** DRAFT, not locked. Lock it in the app only after every `[bracketed]` item is filled in.
**Practitioner:** Mark E. Kantner, Blue Ember Wellness (Practitioner A)
**Version:** 1 (draft, September 27, 2026)

## 1. Question
Does Reiki intention change heart rate variability beyond the same touch given without it?

## 2. Conditions
| Code | Name | Touch | Practitioner intention |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Reiki | Client's locked touch profile | On |
| B | Touch only | Identical to A | Withheld |
| C | Rest | None; practitioner present in the room | Withheld |

## 3. Design
- **Crossover:** each client has one session of each condition, in random order.
- **Allocation:** all six orders of A, B and C, assigned in shuffled blocks of six by the app. The order is hidden from the client, and revealed to the practitioner one visit at a time, only after the before-reading.
- **Sessions:** at least [N] days apart.
- **Target:** 12 clients (36 sessions).

## 4. Outcomes
- **Primary:** change in HRV (rMSSD), before → after. Measured with a 60-second on-table camera reading, face-up, after 2 minutes of settling. Compared as A minus B, within each client.
- **Secondary:** B minus C (the effect of touch alone). Also the client's 0–10 relaxation rating, before → after.
- **Quality checks:**
  - The client's guess ("Which do you think you received?"), answered on their own phone.
  - The practitioner's 0–10 rating of how fully the assigned intention was held.
  - Drift count.
  - Deviations from protocol.

## 5. Session order
1. Greeting, spoken. **No handshake or other touch** outside the touch profile.
2. Client lies face-up; 2-minute settle.
3. Before-reading (60 seconds).
4. Eye pillow on. The practitioner opens the assignment in the app and sets the condition silently.
5. Session: 12 positions on a 5-minute chime. Same room, music and lighting every time.
6. After-reading, face-up, eye pillow still on.
7. Client's relaxation rating and guess, on the client's phone.
8. **Closing Reiki, about 5 minutes, for every client in every condition** (after all measurements).
9. Practitioner records: checklist, intention-held rating, drift count, deviations, notes.

## 6. Withholding procedure (practitioner's own words)
*Shown in the app before every B and C session. The protocol can't be locked while this is empty.*

> Setting the condition (after the eye pillow is on, about ten seconds, same posture every time):
> - **A:** [what you do, silently, to offer Reiki]
> - **B and C:** "Okay, turn off my Reiki transmission."
>
> At each 5-minute chime: [A: silently reaffirm on / B and C: repeat the off phrase].
>
> On noticing drift during B or C: repeat the off phrase, and add one to the drift count after the session.

## 7. Practitioner's commitment
> I can withhold intention. If A and B don't differ, I'll conclude that intention didn't measurably change this outcome, and present it that way.

## 8. Known limits (state these openly in any presentation)
- **The practitioner isn't blind to the condition.** Unconscious differences in touch or presence are possible. The chime-timed positions and the client's guess are the checks for this.
- **Withholding can't be verified from outside.** The drift count and the intention-held rating are the record.
- **HRV over one session is a single outcome.** A null result on HRV doesn't show that Reiki has no effect of any kind.
- **Small sample from a single practice.** These are findings from our sessions, not general claims.
