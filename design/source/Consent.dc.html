Join a session study

Your practitioner is studying whether Reiki intention changes heart rate variability beyond the same touch given without it. Here's exactly what that means for you.

Three visits, three kinds of session

1Reiki with your usual touch
2The same touch, with your practitioner deliberately not offering Reiki
3Rest on the table, no touch, practitioner in the room

The order is random. Every session has the same room, length, music and lighting. Study sessions are [YOUR STUDY PRICE].

What you won't be told

You'll know whether a session has touch. You won't be told whether a session with touch was Reiki or touch alone until the study ends. After each one, you'll be asked which you think it was.

You set the touch

Before your first visit you'll choose where touch is welcome and where you'd prefer hovering hands. That profile stays the same for every session, and there's no touch outside it, not even a handshake.

What you'll do

Lie face-up for a 60-second fingertip reading on your phone just before and just after each session. Then rate how relaxed you feel and say which session you think it was.

What's shared, and with whom

Only your on-table readings and answers go to your practitioner, under a code, not your name. Your daily sleep, screen and stillness data stay yours unless you choose to share them. Results may be presented in summary, never in a way that identifies you.

You're in control

Leave any time, for any reason, and delete your study data with one tap. Leaving doesn't change your care. This is not medical care and doesn't replace it.
